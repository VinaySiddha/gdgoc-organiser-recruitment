# 📁 Starter Templates & Candidate Workspace

Welcome to the `starter/` directory. This directory provides boilerplate templates and configuration templates to help you structure your submission and disclosures efficiently.

---

## 📄 Included Templates

| Template File | Purpose | Instructions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [`SUBMISSION.md`](SUBMISSION.md) | Official assessment submission dossier | Fill in all fields with your details, project overview, architectural choices, and self-evaluation. |
| [`AI_DISCLOSURE.md`](AI_DISCLOSURE.md) | AI transparency and attribution log | Document all AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, etc.) and prompts/workflows utilized. |
| [`DEBUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`](DEBUG_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) | Root-Cause Analysis (RCA) template for Challenge 01 | Copy to `assessment/challenge-01-debugging/DEBUG_REPORT.md` and complete your RCA. |

---

## 🚀 How to Use These Templates

1. **Keep copies or fill them in-place:** You may fill out `SUBMISSION.md` and `AI_DISCLOSURE.md` directly inside `starter/` or copy them to the root directory of your repository.
2. **Do not remove sections:** Keep all headers and prompts intact. If a section is not applicable, state `N/A` and briefly explain why.
3. **Be honest and thorough:** High-quality documentation and self-reflection are heavily weighted in the organizer selection rubric.
